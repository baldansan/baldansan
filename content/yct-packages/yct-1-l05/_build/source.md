# YCT 1 · Lesson 5 · 我6岁。 (I'm 6 years old.) — source (book pp.19–23; page images p-21..p-25)

## Key sentences (p.19)
- Nǐ jǐ suì? 你几岁？ How old are you?
- Nǐ gēge duōdà? 你哥哥多大？ How old is your big brother?

## Let's learn (CD 05-01) — p.19
Number grid 1–100 (digits only; blank middle cells to fill in). Tasks: 1. Complete the form, count 1 to 100. 2. How old are you? Circle the number and tell your partner.
- 岁 suì — year(s) old: 几岁
- *多大 duōdà — how old: 你多大？
- *也 yě — also, too: 他也6岁。
(* = starred/extended words)

## Let's sing (CD 05-03) — 你几岁？ p.21
老师：玛丽，玛丽，你几岁？ (Mǎlì, Mǎlì, nǐ jǐ suì?)
玛丽：8岁，8岁，我8岁。 (Bā suì, bā suì, wǒ bā suì.)
老师：大卫，大卫，你几岁？ (Dàwèi, Dàwèi, nǐ jǐ suì?)
大卫：9岁，9岁，我9岁。 (Jiǔ suì, jiǔ suì, wǒ jiǔ suì.)
玛丽/大卫：老师，老师，您多大？ (Lǎoshī, lǎoshī, nín duōdà?)
老师：秘密，秘密，是秘密，是～秘～密～。 (Mìmì, mìmì, shì mìmì, shì — mì — mì —.)
Note box: "Replace them with your name." (玛丽/大卫 → own name). 您 nín appears in song (polite you).

## Mini story (CD 05-04) — 我家的相册 Wǒ jiā de xiàngcè (p.22)
4 album photos:
1. twin babies in strollers: 弟弟1岁，妹妹也1岁。 Dìdi yí suì, mèimei yě yí suì.
2. boy with backpack, book: 我7岁。 Wǒ qī suì.
3. wedding couple: 爸爸35岁，妈妈也35岁。 Bàba sānshíwǔ suì, māma yě sānshíwǔ suì.
4. elderly couple at Great Wall: 爷爷60岁，奶奶也60岁。 Yéye liùshí suì, nǎinai yě liùshí suì.
Task: imagine your whole life, draw it in four pictures, write your age in Chinese on each.
