# YCT 4 · Lesson 1 — 我们有一百零八个学生。 (We have 108 students.)  [ном х.3–7; pdf p-10..p-14; warm-up х.1–2 = pdf p-08, p-09]

## х.3 Key sentences
- Wǒmen yǒu yì bǎi líng bā ge xuésheng. 我们有一百零八个学生。 We have 108 students.
- Wǒmen xuéxiào yǒu yì qiān bā bǎi duō ge xuésheng. 我们学校有一千八百多个学生。 There are more than 1 800 students in our school.

## Let's learn (📀 01-01)
手机 shǒujī mobile phone · 电脑 diànnǎo computer · 零 líng zero · 上网 shàngwǎng go online
百 bǎi hundred (一百, 二百) · 千 qiān thousand (三千, 四千) · 少 shǎo less (太少了)
Tip box: The Price is Right — cards with items (手机) and a price on the back, 4 choices, guess the right price.

## х.4 Let's read (📀 01-02)
Яриа 1 (3 мөр + 4): boy: 你们学校有多少个学生？ / girl: 我们有一百零八个学生。 / boy: 太少了，我们学校有一千八百多个学生。 / girl: 一千八百？太多了！
Questions: 女孩的学校有多少个学生？男孩的学校呢？
Яриа 2: son: 爸爸，我想买一个新电脑，还想买一个新手机，我的手机不能上网。/ dad: 你有多少钱？/ son: 我有二十块，电脑和手机要一千二百块。/ dad: 你有了一千二百块再说吧。
Questions: 他要买什么？要多少钱？  (Do you know how much a computer and mobile phone usually cost?)
EN (tr-69): 1) Boy: How many students in your school? Girl: 108. Boy: So few. >1800 in ours. Girl: 1800? So many! 2) Son wants new computer and phone, phone can't go online. Dad: how much money? Son: 20 kuai; computer+phone cost 1200 kuai. Dad: talk when you have 1200.

## х.5 Let's count and say / Let's DIY
Count and say: phone row: 10 _ 30 _ _ / _ _ _ _ 100 (10,20…100); laptop row: 100 _ _ _ _ / _ 700 _ _ 1000 (100…1000).
DIY: design a price tag for a computer or mobile phone, recommend product to classmates.
