- **Source:** Tatoeba Project, https://tatoeba.org/ — weekly per-language exports from https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/ (fetched 2026-09-26): `cmn_sentences_detailed`, `cmn_transcriptions`, `cmn-eng_links`, `cmn-mon_links`, `cmn_sentences_CC0`, `eng_sentences_detailed` (only linked English rows are used), `mon_sentences_detailed`.
- **License:** Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (CC BY 2.0 FR), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/. Tatoeba marks a small subset CC0; at this export only one cmn sentence is CC0, and it is not among the kept rows. Each item carries its own `license`.
- **Required attribution:**
  > Example sentences from the Tatoeba Project (https://tatoeba.org/), by Tatoeba contributors, licensed CC BY 2.0 FR (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). Changes: traditional sentences shown in Tatoeba's simplified transcription; pinyin converted from numbered to tone marks.
- **Per-sentence attribution requirement:** CC BY means **every sentence we display must be traceable to its author**. Each item keeps the Tatoeba sentence id (`id`, link `https://tatoeba.org/sentences/show/<id>`) and the contributor username (`author`), plus `en_id`/`en_author` (or `mn_id`/`mn_author`) for the translation. The ready-made `attribution` string is `Tatoeba #<id> by <user> (<link>), CC BY 2.0 FR`. Do not drop these fields. Show them in the UI (e.g. a small "Tatoeba #id · user" link) or on the credits page.
- **Audio:** not ingested. Most Tatoeba cmn audio has no licence for use outside Tatoeba.

### `sentences.json` — `[{id, zh, pinyin, pinyin_src, en, en_id, level, hsk14_ratio, trad_original, author, en_author, source, license, attribution}]`
- `zh` — simplified. 8,367 of the kept sentences were written in traditional script on Tatoeba; for these, `zh` is Tatoeba's own **Hans transcription** (`trad_original: true`).
- `pinyin` — from Tatoeba's **Latn transcription** (numbered, word-segmented), converted to tone marks, e.g. `Wǒmen shìshi kàn!`. `pinyin_src`: `tatoeba-reviewed` (3,508, checked by a Tatoeba user) or `tatoeba-auto` (16,492, machine-generated by Tatoeba, not reviewed). The pypinyin fallback was never needed.
- `en` — the lowest-id English sentence directly linked to the Chinese one.
- `level` — estimated from `data/hsk_words.json`: the highest level among the words (forward maximum matching). Distribution: 1: 4,331, 2: 3,896, 3: 7,472, 4: 4,301.
- `hsk14_ratio` — share of Han characters that belong to HSK 1–4 words in `data/hsk_words.json`.

### `zh-mn.json` — `[{id, zh, pinyin, pinyin_src, mn, mn_id, level, author, mn_author, source, license, attribution}]`
All 21 Tatoeba cmn↔mon links. The Mongolian text (`mn`) is Tatoeba's own human translation. It is not ours and is unreviewed. No length or level filter was applied.

## Selection (script: `python3 scripts/open/tatoeba-cmn.py`)
Of 89,102 cmn sentences:
- 22,973 had no English translation, so they were skipped.
- 3 had no Han characters, so they were skipped.
- 2,458 had more than 20 Han characters, so they were skipped.
- 1,697 contained Latin letters or digits (names such as "Tom", numbers), so they were skipped.
- 20,660 had less than 90 % of their Han characters inside the HSK 1–4 character set (from `data/hsk_words.json`), so they were skipped.
- 372 were duplicates of an already kept simplified text, so they were skipped.
- **40,939 candidates remained.** We kept 20,000, sorted by lowest `level` first and then the shortest sentence. The other 20,939 were dropped only because of the cap; most of them are level 4 and longer level-3 sentences. To keep more, raise `MAX_KEEP`.

## Caveats
- `level` depends on `data/hsk_words.json`, whose bands differ a lot from the official HSK 3.0 (2026) list (see `content/open/zhongdex/README.md`).
- Tatoeba content is crowd-sourced. Some sentences are unnatural, and some English is loose. Auto pinyin writes 一/不 without tone sandhi and may segment words oddly (e.g. `dǎdiànhuàgěi`). Teachers should check 多音字.
