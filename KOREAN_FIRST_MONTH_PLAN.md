# Korean Book 1 — First month teaching plan

Course: **`korean-1`** — Монгол хүнд зориулсан Солонгос хэл 1  
Audience: Complete beginner (Mongolian speaker, workplace Korean goal)

---

## Week 1 — Hangul + 소개

**Goal:** Read and write basic Hangul; introduce yourself in simple Korean.

| Day | App lesson | Focus | Offline / teacher |
|-----|------------|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | `k-pre-01` | ㅏ ㅓ ㅗ ㅜ ㅡ ㅣ | Write vowels; read 아–이 aloud |
| 2 | `k-pre-02` | Y/compound vowels | Drill 야 여 요 유, 애 에 |
| 3 | `k-pre-03` | Basic consonants | 가–하 table |
| 4 | `k-pre-04` | Tense + aspirated | Contrast ㄱ/ㄲ/ㅋ, ㅂ/ㅃ/ㅍ |
| 5 | `k-pre-05` | Syllable building | Build 10 CV blocks |
| 6 | `k-pre-06` | 받침 | Read 한국, 몽골, 밥, 물, 집 |
| 7 | `k-pre-07` + `k-pre-08` | Reading + final test | Pass Hangul quiz ≥ 70% |

**End of week 1:** Start **`k-01` 소개** — 안녕하세요, 입니다, name/country/job.

---

## Week 2 — Basic workplace instructions

**Goal:** Understand and respond to simple commands and questions at work.

| Day | Content | Topics |
|-----|---------|--------|
| 8–9 | `k-01` (continued) | 입니까, 은/는, polite greetings |
| 10 | `k-02` 학교 (partial) | 여기/거기, 이/가, location words |
| 11–12 | Workplace phrase pack (future JSON) | 오세요, 앉으세요, 잠깐만요 |
| 13–14 | Review + quiz | Week 2 vocabulary games |

**Skills:** Yes/no (네/아니요), “where is…?”, “this/that”.

---

## Week 3 — Daily life survival

**Goal:** Handle food, time, numbers, and basic needs.

| Day | Content | Topics |
|-----|---------|--------|
| 15–16 | Daily life vocab (future) | 밥, 물, 화장실, 시간 |
| 17–18 | Numbers + time | 일–십, 몇 시 |
| 19–20 | Shopping / transport phrases | 얼마예요?, 주세요 |
| 21 | Review games | Match + Translate from Weeks 1–3 |

**Skills:** Order food, ask price, tell time (intro level).

---

## Week 4 — Workplace roleplay and test

**Goal:** Short roleplays: introduction, asking for help, simple task confirmation.

| Day | Content | Topics |
|-----|---------|--------|
| 22–23 | Roleplay A | Meet coworker; state name + job |
| 24–25 | Roleplay B | Ask where something is; thank you / sorry |
| 26–27 | Mixed review | Hangul + L01 + L02 quiz |
| 28–30 | Month test | Oral + app quiz; plan Month 2 |

**Success criteria:**

- Read simple Hangul words (한국, 학교, 사람)
- Self-intro: 이름, 국적, 직업 with 입니다
- 20+ survival words active recall
